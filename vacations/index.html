What is guestpix?

GUESTPIX™ is your digital vacation album that un-complicates the issue of receiving hundreds (or thousands) of photos, videos and messages from your travel group.

Forget checking your chat apps, emails and SMS for photos then saving them all one at a time. The GUESTPIX™ holiday photo sharing album is a secure way to collect the best memories from your group that does all the hard work for you.

HOW GUESTPIX WORKS

It only takes 2 minutes to set up!

The easy way to collect vacation photos and videos from your group.

how it works

You and your group can...

1000's photos & videos

Upload unlimited photos and videos from your travel group. You’ll love exploring your holiday through the eyes of everyone on tour.

photo & video slideshow

Activate the photo & video slideshow feature and enjoy seeing all the highlights! Connect to a projector, chromecast or screen mirror technology. It’s a great idea to use and enjoy on the last night of your trip.

guestbook messages

The written and video guestbook allows your travel group to upload messages as a way to remember those special moments and little details you never want to forget.

From us you'll receive...

QR code, private link & pin

Your custom QR code / private link (with PIN) is your ticket to receiving and uploading content from your travel group. No guest registration and no app to download. Too easy!

digital album

The group will love the convenience of all their holiday memories saved in one digital album. Everyone can add titles and descriptions to their uploaded photos. It’s so easy to tap the private link and upload.

zip download

Ready for everyone to download the holiday album? Everything is neatly zipped and ready to download within 12 months of your holiday. Toggle on the ‘Guest Download’ setting so your travel companions can download the zip from the guest portal. It’s the perfect way to save the memories forever.

More About GUESTPIX™ Vacations

Traveling is all about making memories, and there’s no better way to relive those moments than through a beautifully curated family photo album made with GUESTPIX™. Whether you’re exploring a new city, relaxing on a beach, or venturing into the great outdoors, capturing the highlights of your journey allows you to revisit those special experiences for years to come. A well-organized travel photo album turns those snapshots into a lasting keepsake, preserving the essence of each destination and the joy of the adventure.

For many travelers, putting together a travel photo book is a creative and meaningful way to document their experiences. From breathtaking landscapes to candid moments with loved ones, a thoughtfully arranged photo book brings your trip to life. Whether you’re collecting vacation pictures from a solo getaway, a romantic retreat, or a family holiday, using GUESTPIX™ to turn them into a visual story makes your travel memories even more special.

One of the best parts of any trip is capturing the perfect picture of the trip, that one standout image that sums up the adventure. Whether it’s a stunning sunset, a historic landmark, or a fun group selfie, travel photography allows you to freeze those unforgettable moments in time. If you love to travel the world, pictures become the gateway to share your experiences with others, inspire future adventures, and reflect on the incredible places you’ve visited.

Road trips, in particular, are filled with unique photo opportunities. With scenic highways and quirky roadside attractions, road trip photos tell the story of the journey just as much as the destination. Capturing candid moments along the way, like spontaneous pit stops and unexpected discoveries, adds personality and depth to your travel collection.

No matter, if you’re a seasoned traveler or someone who simply enjoys the occasional getaway, organizing your travel photos into albums or digital galleries, GUESTPIX™ ensures that your memories remain easily accessible and beautifully presented. A well-curated collection of vacation snapshots allows you to relive the excitement, beauty, and emotions of your travels long after you’ve returned home.

No matter how you celebrate, thoughtful planning and creative ideas help make every holiday gathering special. From invitations and games to memorable photos, bringing people together is what the season is all about!
