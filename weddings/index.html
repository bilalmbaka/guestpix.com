Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Which Package Is Right for My Wedding?

Most couples go with The Signature Package, as it gives you everything you need to create a beautiful, personalized wedding photo gallery:

  • Choice of 20 design themes to match your wedding aesthetic
  • Unlimited Super Albums to organise photos by moment (ceremony, reception, after party, and more)
  • 12-month upload window so guests and photographers can keep contributing long after the wedding day
  • 1 Digital Invitation and RSVP included


The Signature Bundle is perfect if you are planning multiple events like an engagement party, rehearsal dinner, and wedding, as it includes 3 separate event galleries, each with its own QR code and albums.

The Classic Package is a great starting point if you need a simple, no-fuss way to collect and share wedding photos with a 3-month upload window.

Still not sure? Compare all packages here, or get in touch, we are happy to help you find the perfect fit.

Is GUESTPIX™ a One-Time Payment or a Subscription?

GUESTPIX™ is a one-time payment with no recurring charges, no hidden fees, and no auto-renewal. You pay once and your gallery is live and hosted for 12 months from your event date.

If you would like to keep your gallery online beyond the 12-month period, you have the option to renew at a discounted rate. Completely optional, no surprises.

Can Guests Upload Photos After the Wedding Day?

Yes, and it happens more than you might expect. Guests often find their best shots in the days and even weeks after the event. With the Signature and Luxe packages, your upload window stays open for a full 12 months from your event date so no moment gets missed.

Even your professional photographer can be given access to upload their shots directly into a dedicated album, keeping everything beautifully organized in one place.

Do My Guests Need to Download an App?

No app download required. Guests simply scan your QR code or tap your private link on any device and they will land straight in your gallery through their browser. No sign-ups, no passwords, no friction on the day for your guests.

This means even your least tech-savvy guests can upload their photos and videos in seconds, right from the dance floor.

Are My Photos Private and Safe?

Your gallery is completely private. Only guests with your unique QR code or private link can access it. GUESTPIX™ uses enterprise-grade security, and 100% of your content copyright stays with you. We will never sell, use, or share your photos for any purpose.

You are also in full control of your gallery at all times, with the ability to manage guest access and moderate content from your host dashboard.

When Should I Purchase My Gallery?

You can purchase whenever suits you, whether that is 12 months out or the week before your wedding. Your upload window and 12-month hosting period start from your event date, not your purchase date, so there is no risk in buying early.

In fact, buying ahead of time is a great idea. It gives you time to download and customise your QR code, print it onto table cards and signage using our 180+ free Canva templates, and have everything ready well before the big day.

More About GUESTPIX™ Weddings

A wedding guest book has long been a cherished tradition, allowing couples to capture heartfelt messages from loved ones. But in today’s digital world, there’s a new way to preserve wedding memories. Instead of a traditional book filled with signatures, why not create an interactive experience where guests can share their favorite moments in real-time? With a GUESTPIX™ QR code for wedding pictures, collecting snapshots from family and friends has never been easier.

By using a wedding QR code for photos, guests can instantly upload and access pictures from the big day. Whether they’re candid shots, group selfies, or behind-the-scenes moments, this modern approach ensures no memory goes unnoticed. It’s a simple, hassle-free way to compile a beautiful collection of wedding party photos while letting guests participate in the storytelling of your special day.

Your wedding album is one of the most treasured keepsakes from your celebration. Whilst a professional wedding photographer captures the formal portraits and key moments, images and moments captured through your guest’s eyes, add a personal, unfiltered touch to your collection. The combination of professional wedding photography and guest-captured images helps tell your story from every perspective.

For couples who love a hands-on approach, a DIY photo booth is a fantastic addition to any reception. Set up a fun backdrop, add some props, and let guests take creative, candid pictures throughout the night. Pairing a photo booth setup with the GUESTPIX™ sharing system ensures every image is easily accessible, making it effortless to compile a private digital photo gallery of all the fun.

Whether you’re opting for a traditional wedding guest book, an interactive QR code sharing system, or both, the goal remains the same: preserving memories in a way that feels meaningful and effortless. Modern wedding technology makes it easy to bring everyone together, ensuring that every heartfelt smile, joyful tear, and spontaneous dance move is captured and shared.
