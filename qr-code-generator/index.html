Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Is this QR code generator really free?

Absolutely! We provide this as a completely free service, no strings attached. All we ask is that you TEST the QR Code first and ensure it points to the right location.

Do you offer a free photo sharing plan?

Your free GUESTPIX event includes a unique QR code and link, up to 50 photos, digital invitations and RSVP, unlimited guests, social reactions and comments, and a zip download of all your photos. Your gallery is open for uploads immediately. 

Can I use a QR code to collect wedding photos from guests?

Yes. Point your QR code at a GUESTPIX gallery and guests can upload their photos and videos instantly, no app required. GUESTPIX has powered photo sharing at more than 200,000 events worldwide.

How long does my free gallery last?

Your gallery is open for uploads immediately and stays active for uploads and viewing for 30 days after your event date. Your gallery will be permanently deleted after 90 days. You can upgrade or extend your gallery at any time.

Can I upgrade later if I want more features?

Yes and it’s instant. Upgrading removes the photo limit immediately, unlocks video uploads, gallery customization, albums, and more. Your existing photos and event details stay exactly as they are.

What's not included in the free plan?

Video uploads, gallery customisation (themes and colours), albums, and a welcome video. These are all available on our paid plans.

Do the QR codes expire?

No. These are static QR codes that encode your link directly, so they work forever. Many other free generators create dynamic codes that stop working when a trial ends.

How do I download my photos?

You can download your entire gallery as a ZIP file (including full-resolution photos, organized by guest name, and a CSV of guestbook messages) instead of saving items one by one.

  1. Log in at my.guestpix.com and go to the Export section
  2. Click Generate ZIP under Quick Links
  3. Once generated, you’ll receive an email with your download link

Tips: Make sure you have enough storage space, download to a computer rather than a phone, and download before your free period ends.
