Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

My clients are already asking about guest photo sharing, is GUESTPIX the right solution?

If your clients are asking, they have likely already heard of us. GUESTPIX is the platform that pioneered QR code photo sharing for events and remains the most trusted globally, used at 150,000 + events worldwide. It is a no-app experience, so every guest can participate regardless of age or tech conform. With full-resolution uploads, real-time sharing, and a beautifully designed private gallery, it’s an easy, reliable solution you can feel confident recommending.

What are Pro Bundles and how do they work?

Pro Bundles allow you to purchase galleries upfront at an exclusive partner rate. You can buy in bulk and save up to 40% off retail, giving you the flexibility to include galleries in your packages and either absorb the cost as a value add or mark up to retail. You simply use your galleries whenever you need them – creating and assigning them to clients as bookings come in.

How do I include GUESTPIX in my packages?

Most professionals build the gallery cost into their package pricing and offer it as an inclusion. You purchase at your bundle rate, set up the gallery for your client, and deliver it as part of your service. Some professionals prefer to offer it as a premium add-on – either works.

Do I set up the gallery for my client, or do they do it themselves?

It’s completely up to you. You can create and manage the gallery on your client’s behalf or transfer it to them, so they have full control. Most professionals prefer to set it up and transfer it, so the client receives something that already looks polished and feels premium. Either way, it takes under two minutes to create a gallery.

Will it feel like my brand, or will guests know it's a GUESTPIX product?

Every gallery includes complimentary white labelling. The experience your clients and their guests see is a seamless, branded gallery that feels like a natural extension of your service and showcases your brand or venue. Your gallery represents you – with your colors, logo, social handles and website links, all in a beautiful, private gallery.

Can I customise the gallery to match my client's wedding aesthetic?

Yes, every gallery comes with a choice of 20 design themes and customization options, so each gallery feels personal to your client’s event. You can set this up yourself or let your client personalize it with their event aesthetic.

What do my clients' guests experience, and will they use it?

Guests simply scan a QR code or tap a private link – no app download or account registration required. They add their name, upload photos, videos, or a guestbook message, and instantly see everything in the shared gallery. It works on any device, anywhere in the world. Events using GUESTPIX see on average 2x more photos and 5x more video uploads compared to standard sharing methods. Your clients will wake up the next morning to a gallery already filling up and will thank you for recommending it.

See our tips to maximize guest engagement and content capture in the Partner Help Center.

Do my galleries expire if I don't use them straight away?

No, your bundle galleries are yours to use when you need them. There’s no pressure to use them by a certain date, so you can buy with confidence and work through your bundle at your own pace.
